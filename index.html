01 / About

The journey behind the dashboards.

My path into data has been less about following a perfect roadmap and more about learning, building, and finding better questions to ask.

I started with Computer Science at the Air Force Institute of Technology, where I built a foundation in programming, algorithms, databases, operating systems, networks, and software engineering.

After school, I moved deeper into data: cleaning imperfect datasets, building dashboards that answer business questions, automating repetitive reporting, and learning how to explain findings beyond the spreadsheet.

A dashboard is not the destination. The decision it helps someone make is.
What I am building toward

Analytics today. Data science and responsible AI tomorrow.

My current focus is entry-level and junior data analytics work, especially roles where I can combine technical analysis with business, process, and customer understanding.

I’m also expanding my data science and machine learning skill set, with a growing interest in AI ethics, explainability, and culturally situated fairness in global AI systems.
